AE2 Channel Tools includes code adapted from Applied-Energistics-2-Unofficial:
https://github.com/GTNewHorizons/Applied-Energistics-2-Unofficial

The channel placement tracker, channel loss filters, highlight renderer, capacity calculation,
and associated regression tests were adapted from the local channel-loss-alerts and
network-visualiser-free-channels branches. Block outline rendering derives from AE2's
BlockPosHighlighter (which credits McJty's McJtyLib).
